# AsanKana

## Family Location Tracking & Emergency Response App

AsanKana is a modern safety-focused mobile application designed to help families, trusted contacts, and communities stay connected through real-time location sharing and emergency response tools.

Built for modern-day safety and peace of mind, AsanKana allows users to instantly notify loved ones during emergencies, dangerous situations, accidents, or unexpected incidents.

---

# Vision

In today’s world, knowing where your loved ones are and whether they are safe has become increasingly important.

AsanKana aims to become a reliable personal safety companion that improves emergency communication, reduces response time, and provides peace of mind for families and individuals.

---

# MVP Goals

The goal of the MVP is to create a lightweight but reliable emergency and location-sharing platform that focuses on:

* Real-time safety awareness
* Faster emergency communication
* Simple and accessible emergency tools
* Family-centered protection
* Quick response coordination

---

# Core Features

## Real-time GPS Tracking

Track live user locations securely and accurately.

## Family & Trusted Groups

Create private groups for family members, friends, or trusted contacts.

## One-click SOS Button

Send emergency alerts instantly with one tap.

## Live Location Sharing

Share current location during emergencies or travel.

## Emergency Notifications

Notify trusted contacts immediately during critical situations.

## Safe Zones & Geofence Alerts

Receive alerts when users enter or leave designated areas.

## Incident History Logs

Maintain emergency activity and alert records.

## Emergency Contact Management

Manage emergency contacts and trusted people easily.

## Battery-efficient Tracking

Optimized GPS tracking designed to minimize battery usage.

## Quick Response Interface

Simple and accessible interface for urgent situations.
